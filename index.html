About Tongsum

A Celebration of Harvest and Gratitude

Tongsum is a harvesting festival associated with the Limboo community. The festival reflects the importance of agriculture, nature and the traditional way of life.

It is a time to celebrate the harvest, express gratitude for nature's blessings and appreciate the cultural traditions passed down through generations.

Tongsum represents the connection between people, their land, their agricultural practices and their cultural identity.

Darap Wellness Tourism

A 4-Day Journey Through Nature, Wellness and Sikkimese Culture

Darap Wellness Tourism is a soulful journey into the serene landscapes and living traditions of Sikkim. Set in the peaceful village of Darap, this experience brings together mindful wellness, nature, local food and authentic community life.

From gentle walks and natural water sound therapy to herbal gardens, farm-to-table meals and Limboo cultural experiences, every moment invites you to slow down, reconnect and experience Sikkim with heart.
